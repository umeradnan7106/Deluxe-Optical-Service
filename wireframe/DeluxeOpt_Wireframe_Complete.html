Blue Cut Lenses —
Complete Guide

Everything you need to know about blue light filtering lenses and why you might need them.

What is Blue Light?

Blue light is a high-energy visible light emitted by digital screens, LED lighting, and the sun. Prolonged exposure can cause digital eye strain, headaches, and disrupted sleep patterns.

Blue cut lenses have a special filter applied that blocks harmful blue light wavelengths (380-500nm) while allowing beneficial visible light through.

Blue Cut Lens Technology Image

Reduces Eye Strain

After long screen sessions, your eyes feel less tired and dry

Better Sleep

Blue light suppresses melatonin. Filtering it helps you sleep better

Prevents Headaches

Screen-induced headaches reduce significantly with blue cut lenses
