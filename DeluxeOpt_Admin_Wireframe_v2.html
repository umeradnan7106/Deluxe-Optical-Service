What are Blue Cut Lenses?

Blue cut lenses (also called blue light blocking lenses) are specially coated eyeglass lenses that filter out harmful blue-violet light emitted by digital screens, smartphones, and LED lighting.

Who Needs Blue Cut Lenses?

If you spend more than 4–6 hours daily in front of a screen — whether for work, gaming, or entertainment — blue cut lenses can significantly benefit your eye health and sleep quality...
